When somewhere doesn’t feel like home, it’s almost never the building’s fault. A flat that ticked every box on paper still didn’t work. A beautiful, empty hostel didn’t either. For a few women specifically, feeling genuinely safe, not just comfortable, has been the deciding factor – and one participant’s genuinely hard physical day (illness, heat, a dying phone) didn’t dent her sense of belonging at all, because the people around her didn’t let her down.
