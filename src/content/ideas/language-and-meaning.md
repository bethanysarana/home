The linguistic findings in Section 3.7 sit within the broader, long-standing, and still-debated tradition of linguistic relativity - the proposition that the structure of a language can shape, without strictly determining, the thought and experience of its speakers. This project's findings offer only a small, qualitative, and non-representative illustration of this idea rather than a test of it, and should be read accordingly: three participants, three language pairs, one consistent pattern. It is nonetheless a striking enough convergence to be worth deliberately testing with a direct question in future conversations, rather than relying on it to surface incidentally as it has so far.
