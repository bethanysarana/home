For every other participant, find-or-create is a live, answerable question. For Eduardo it is not, because the place it would refer to no longer exists. His response withdraws from the premise entirely.

> *“I think I prefer not to call something home or belong, for not to lose it again.” — Eduardo*

The same pattern recurs in physical objects: a car-cleaning rag used as a towel, and the same hiking backpack, for over a decade, chosen for their impermanence.

> *“Maybe that's the reason why I don't have a towel and I use a travelling towel... I'm still not in my home.” — Eduardo, on a car-cleaning rag used for over a decade*


Notably, Eduardo has also taken concrete, practical steps toward settling - a residential lease and citizenship in Australia - which he explicitly discounts as not constituting home. The pattern across his account is therefore not an absence of stability, but a considered, repeated refusal to apply the word ‘home’ to anything he currently has access to.
