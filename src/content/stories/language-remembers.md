Among the genuinely multilingual participants, home is not only described differently across languages, but appears to be structured differently by the vocabulary each language makes available.

> *“In Polish, there is no distinguishing. There is one word.” — Karolina*
>
> *Spanish distinguishes casa (house) from hogar (home), like English, but French, her most dominant language, does not. — Cloe, trilingual*
>
> Michel's account extends this pattern further: asked directly what home is, he answered with a category the question had not offered - language itself, describing the physical relief of overhearing Swedish at an airport as his clearest available marker of arrival.
>
> *“I would say it's like the whole language thing. Language, I would say, is home.” — Michel, on the physical relief of overhearing Swedish at an airport*
